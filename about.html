Her Story

Early Life, Education & Legal Foundation

Mrs. Diiarau Didi Esther Walson-Jack, OON, mni is a distinguished Nigerian lawyer, public administrator, reformer, and author whose career spans 37 years of uninterrupted public service across state and federal tiers of government.

Born on 27 August 1966 in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, her ancestral roots lie in Obololi community in Southern Ijaw Local Government Area of Bayelsa State — a Niger Delta identity she has carried with immense pride. She attended primary school at Rumukrushi and St. Paul's in Port Harcourt before completing secondary education at Federal Government Girls' College, Benin City, and Federal Government College, Okrika.

She earned her Bachelor of Laws (LL.B) from the renowned University of Lagos and was called to the Nigerian Bar in 1987. She further qualified as a Certified Legislative Drafter, mastering the structural mechanics of statutory drafting and executive administration.

Public Leadership

State Leadership & Federal Permanent Secretary Roles

In 1992, she began her government legal career as State Counsel in the Rivers State Ministry of Justice. Following the creation of Bayelsa State in 1996, she became a pioneer staff member of the Bayelsa State Ministry of Justice — undertaking the rare legal responsibility of building state legal frameworks from a clean slate.

Over the subsequent decade, she rose through Bayelsa State's public service to hold three major executive offices: Solicitor-General & Permanent Secretary in the Ministry of Justice, Pioneer Commissioner for Science, Technology and Manpower Development, and Chief of Staff to the Governor of Bayelsa State.

Transitioning to the Federal Civil Service in 2009, she was appointed Federal Permanent Secretary in 2017 — leading administrative execution across five federal ministries: Service Welfare, Power, Water Resources & Sanitation, Niger Delta Affairs, and Education.
