About Her

34 Years of Public Service

Her Public Service Journey

A graduate of the University of Lagos, Mrs. Walson-Jack completed the Nigerian Law School in 1987 and began as State Counsel in Akwa Ibom State during her National Youth Service. After private practice in Port Harcourt she joined the Rivers State Ministry of Justice as State Counsel in 1992.

In Bayelsa State she headed legal drafting for the Ministry of Justice and the House of Assembly, served as Solicitor-General and Permanent Secretary from 2002 to 2009, and later as Commissioner for Science, Technology and Manpower Development and Chief of Staff to the Governor.

She joined the Federal Civil Service in 2009 and was appointed Federal Permanent Secretary in 2017, leading the Service Welfare Office and the Ministries of Niger Delta Affairs, Power, Water Resources and Sanitation, and Education. She served as the 20th Head of the Civil Service of the Federation from 14 August 2024 to August 2026, when she handed over and retired from active public service.

Leadership Values

Principles That Guide Her Work

Core values articulated by Mrs. Walson-Jack to promote integrity, hard work, and excellence in public service.

H
Hardwork
Disciplined, thorough effort visible before the workday officially begins — results earned through consistent work, not proximity to power.
I
Integrity
Non-negotiable ethical standards and zero tolerance for misconduct across every posting and grade level.
D
Diligence
Closing the gap between policy on paper and results in practice through unglamorous follow-through.
E
Empathy
Recognizing that every policy decision eventually reaches a human face — people-centred governance above administrative rigidity.
