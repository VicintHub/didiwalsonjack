Appointed 14 August 2024 by the President of Nigeria with a constitutional mandate for establishment matters. Leading the Federal Civil Service Strategy and Implementation Plan (FCSSIP25) under the theme: Delivering Results — Our Year of Accomplishment.

ECMS Digitalisation (38 MDAs)

Expanded digital work processes from 4 to 38 Ministries & MDAs on 30 Dec 2025, transitioning the Federal Civil Service to paperless, audited workflows.

ServiceWise GPT & GovMail

Trained AI assistant on public service rules & circulars; deployed GovMail to over 115,000 Public Servants at zero extra cost.

Staff Welfare & Housing Act 2026

Enacted Housing Loans Act 2026, Exit Benefit Scheme from Jan 2026, 100% Training DTA, ECA 2010 Compensation & Group Life Assurance.
