Head of the Civil Service of the Federation

Download Legacy Report (.docx)

Appointed by President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, GCFR, in July 2024, Mrs. Walson-Jack served as Head of the Civil Service of the Federation from 14 August 2024 to August 2026, when she handed over and retired from active public service. She led the final stretch of the Federal Civil Service Strategy and Implementation Plan 2021–2025 (FCSSIP25), declared 2025 the Year of Accomplishment, and began the co-creation of its successor, FCSSIP30 (2026–2030).

ECMS Digitalisation (38 MDAs)

Expanded paperless operations from 3 to 38 Ministries, Departments and Agencies on the 1Gov Enterprise Content Management platform, and moved the OHCSF onto the integrated 1Gov Cloud (GovMail, InMail, GovDrive, GovConference) on 11 November 2025.

Service-Wise GPT & GovMail

Introduced Service-Wise GPT, an AI-enabled knowledge platform on public-service rules, circulars and policies, with 75,000+ conversations. GovMail grew from fewer than 20,000 officers in August 2024 to more than 120,000 active accounts.

Staff Welfare Reforms

Secured an exit benefit of 100% of annual emoluments from 1 January 2026, a 40% increase in Peculiar Allowance, a N10 billion annual housing-loan intervention, full Duty Tour Allowance for approved training, and the Employee Compensation Scheme under the ECA 2010.
